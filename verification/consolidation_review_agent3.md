Current `output/writeup.md` and `README.md` contain none of these (grep for `Visa|CBRE|iStar|permco_joins|Low is broader|7 of 12|0-4`: 0 hits in both).
- **Since when / how consolidation made it worse.** The v3 write-up at the tag also lacked them (`git show <tag>:output/writeup.md | grep -c` gives 0), but `output/archive_v1/writeup_v1.md` and `output/archive_v2/writeup_v2.md` carried them (10 and 11 hits; v1 lines 164-194 hold the full text). Consolidation deleted both archives, so the only on-tree copy is gone. It also deleted `output/tables/permco_joins_review.csv`, `handcheck_sample.csv` (the evidence for the user's 10-K hand-check gate) and `coverage_by_sector_year.csv`, all listed in the tag and referenced by CLAUDE.md 8b item 7 and section 9; no live script writes them (`grep -l permco_joins *.py` is empty).
- **Effect.** No number is wrong, but a reader of the single write-up cannot see why the universe excludes Visa/Mastercard, that Low is 0-4, or that the last year is partial.
- **Fix.** Add a "Universe, scoring and data limits" section to `22_writeup.py` (text from `git show <tag>:output/archive_v1/writeup_v1.md` lines 164-194, updated to the literal rule), and either restore the three deleted CSVs from the tag into `output/` with a generating script or say in the write-up that they are in the tag.

- **Fix.** Reword to "fixed in `prereg_main.md` before any run of this pipeline, but after the literal-rule result had been seen in the earlier specification". Add one sentence giving the earlier published net-rule gap (-0.04%) and that the switch moved it to +1.21%.
